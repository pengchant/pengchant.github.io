## Research Interests

My current research interests include:

- Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG)
- Context Compression
- Retrieval Optimization
- Conversational Memory
- AI Agents
- Large Language Model Applications

## Current Topics

### RAG Retrieval Optimization

Exploring how retrieval strategies can improve the accuracy and efficiency of RAG systems.

### Context Compression

Studying methods for reducing long-context information while preserving important semantic information.

### Conversational Memory

Exploring how historical conversations can be summarized, compressed and reused to restore conversational context.

### AI-assisted Teaching

Exploring the combination of RAG and AI Agents with programming education and developer training.
